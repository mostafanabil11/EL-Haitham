## Brand & Style

This design system embodies academic dignity, literary heritage, and modern digital fluidity. Built specifically for an Arabic language educator, the interface bridges classical linguistic authority with modern, approachable pedagogy. It balances serious academic rigor (tailored for secondary school and *Thanaweya Amma* students) with an encouraging, high-clarity environment that eliminates cognitive overload.

### Design Movement & Ethos
The aesthetic merges **Modern Academic Minimalism** with subtle **Neo-Calligraphic Structuralism**:
- **Directionality First:** Native right-to-left (RTL) reading flow, prioritizing natural eye scans from right to left across cards, metrics, and navigation rails.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Clean, unencumbered whitespace allows nuanced Arabic letterforms and diacritics (*harakat*) to breathe without visual clipping.
- **Academic Elevation:** Deep scholarly indigos ground the experience in expertise, while warm amber-gold illuminates student milestones, honor rolls, and literary mastery badges.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Inspires confidence, intellectual pride, clarity under exam preparation pressure, and an accessible mentor-student relationship.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classical parchment illumination, royal sapphire inks, and polished slate, engineered specifically for high-legibility light mode screens.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#16203B`):** Deep scholarly indigo used for high-impact typography, primary interactive actions, key active states, and dominant institutional branding.
- **Secondary Amber Gold (`#C5933A`):** The literary accent. Used for academic grade distinctions, honors (*Awā'il*), completion streaks, certificates, and contextual spotlight actions.
- **Tertiary Warm Teal (`#1E6B7B`):** Balances deep indigo with an energetic pedagogical tone. Serves for lecture series badges, secondary data metrics, and exercise progress arcs.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - **Base Ground (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, cool alabaster ensuring glare-free reading across prolonged study sessions.
  - **Card Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces offering crisp boundary separation from the base ground.
  - **Subtle Trough (`#F1F5F9`):** Inset surfaces, track backgrounds for audio lessons, and deactivated quiz modules.
- **Border Spectrum (`#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1`):** Whispering borders providing architectural structure without visual weight.
- **Feedback Accents:**
  - **Success / Correct (`#15803D`):** Direct answers, mastered grammar modules.
  - **Alert / Revision Required (`#B91C1C`):** Missed exercises, critical exam deadlines.

## Typography

Typographic scale and legibility in Arabic require specialized vertical rhythm. Arabic script demands roughly 20-30% more generous line height than Latin equivalents to preserve diacritical marks (*shaddah*, *tanween*, *kasrah*) without ascender/descender collision.

### Typographic Principles
- **Selected Family:** **IBM Plex Sans** (with fallback rendering configured for IBM Plex Sans Arabic). It offers a deliberate, modern balance between mechanical precision and Kufic structural balance, avoiding the childish roundness of informal fonts.
- **Letter Spacing Neutrality:** Tracking is kept at `0px` across most titles and bodies, as tracking expansion can distort connected Arabic glyph ligatures.
- **Hierarchy for Grammatical Text:** Quotes, poetry lines (*Abyāt Shi'r*), and rhetoric examples employ `body-lg` with `lineHeight: 34px` and a semi-bold weight to visually demarcate study samples from instructional text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid with a mobile-first philosophy, catering to students primarily accessing quizzes, video units, and study summaries via smartphones.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 640px):**
  - Single column fluid layout with `gutter-mobile` (16px) margins.
  - Sticky bottom action bar for primary homework/lecture continuation actions.
  - Grade-level switcher implemented as a horizontal snap-scroll chip strip.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):**
  - 8-column layout with 24px margins and gutters.
  - Side-by-side split for syllabus tracks and content viewer.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):**
  - 12-column layout capped at `container-max` (1152px) to guarantee optimal line lengths for intensive reading (maximum 75-80 characters per line in Arabic).
  - Permanent right-hand academic navigation rail with left-hand content flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined pairing of **tonal surface separation** and **ambient indigo-tinted shadows**, eliminating heavy dark shadows in favor of scholarly clarity.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F8FAFC`. Background canvas, unstacked.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells):** `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a fine 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) with an ambient drop: `0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 32, 59, 0.04), 0px 1px 4px -1px rgba(22, 32, 59, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards, Hover States, Floating Controls):** `0px 8px 20px -3px rgba(22, 32, 59, 0.07), 0px 3px 6px -2px rgba(22, 32, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Grade Selectors):** `0px 20px 32px -6px rgba(22, 32, 59, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(22, 32, 59, 0.06)`.

### Border Discipline
All elevated elements carry a subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0` on white; `#CBD5E1` on highlighted states) to ensure accessibility for students viewing under varied ambient light conditions or on budget mobile screens.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced shape language (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting modern educational software: structured enough for professional clarity, yet soft enough to feel encouraging and un-intimidating.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Micro Elements (Badges, Chips, Small Form Tags):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Medium Components (Inputs, Buttons, Quiz Choice Tiles):** `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Surface Containers (Content Cards, Metric Widgets, Video Frames):** `1rem` (16px).
- **Feature Banners & Promotional Announcement Shelves:** `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Indicators & Circular Controls:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for progress bars, status dots, and icon wrappers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Primary Navy (`#16203B`), text in pure white, `min-height: 48px` (touch-friendly), medium rounded corner (12px). Active state transitions to `#0F172A`.
- **Accent / Milestone:** Filled in Amber Gold (`#C5933A`), text in Navy (`#16203B`) or white depending on contrast validation. Used for "Start Exam" or "Subscribe to Year".
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1.5px solid Navy or Gold with transparent interior, providing tactile feedback on hover/press via a 5% opacity tint.

### Grade Level Chips & Academic Badges
- **Thanaweya Amma / Secondary Levels:** Distinctive badge designs utilizing subtle tinted backgrounds with high-contrast text:
  - *Secondary 1st Year (الصف الأول الثانوي):* Slate/Navy tint background (`#F1F5F9`), Navy border and text.
  - *Secondary 2nd Year (الصف الثاني الثانوي):* Teal tint background (`#F0FDFA`), Teal border and text (`#1E6B7B`).
  - *Secondary 3rd Year (الصف الثالث الثانوي - الشهادة الثانوية):* Warm Amber tint (`#FEFCE8`), Gold border (`#C5933A`), dark amber text (`#854D0E`) with an optional micro star icon.

### Cards & Lesson Units
- **Lesson Card:** White background with a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`. Contains an RTL-aligned lesson sequence number, topic title, estimated study duration, and a clean progress ring or linear track.
- **Grammar & Poetry Reference Box:** Features a 3px solid right border (accented in Gold or Primary Navy) with a `#F8FAFC` tinted body, creating an immediate visual anchor for quotes and pedagogical rules.

### Form Inputs & Quiz Choices
- **Inputs:** `min-height: 48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. On focus: Navy outline with a soft 3px box-shadow halo (`rgba(22, 32, 59, 0.1)`). Placeholder text in soft neutral (`#94A3B8`).
- **Interactive Quiz Option Tiles:** Full-width selectable cards with generous padding (16px), allowing whole-row tapping on mobile. Radio indicator placed naturally at the far right of the card, text flowing right-to-left.

### Progress Indicators & Student Metrics
- **Curriculum Progress Bar:** Dual-tone horizontal track; trough in `#E2E8F0`, fill in `#C5933A` (gold) or `#1E6B7B` (teal). Height: 8px, rounded pill.
- **Attendance & Homework Streak:** Circular badge with an ambient gold glow containing the fire/book glyph and numeral count.
